Frequently Asked Questions

Is Splixa free to use?

Yes. The free plan allows unlimited group expense entries with no daily cap and no ads. It covers up to 2 active groups and 50 personal transactions per calendar month. Splixa Pro removes both limits. See pricing for the full breakdown.

Does Splixa connect to my bank?

No. Splixa does not link to bank accounts or store banking credentials. Data is transmitted securely via TLS with row-level database protection.

What platforms are supported?

Splixa is available on Android across 12 languages. The iOS version is currently under active development.
