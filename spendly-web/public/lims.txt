# Splixa - Social Finance & Expense Splitting App

> Splixa is a modern mobile finance application designed to help friends, roommates, and groups track and split shared expenses effortlessly.

## Core Utility
- **Group Splitting:** Create groups for trips or apartments, log shared expenses, and instantly calculate exact debts.
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Convert and split shared travel expenses seamlessly across different global currencies.
- **Visual Analytics:** View monthly category breakdowns and interactive spending charts.
- **Fair Debts:** Net Summary calculations eliminate awkward money conversations by providing exact settlement figures.

## Target Audience
- Roommates sharing rent, groceries, and utilities.
- Friends organizing group trips and holidays.
- Expat communities and international travelers.

## Security & Privacy
- Bank-level 256-bit AES encryption.
- Zero hidden fees.
- 100% data control with explicit account deletion capabilities.
